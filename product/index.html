The Sun’s Company

Eight worlds, one star, four and a half billion years of orbit. A portrait of the solar system as a single family, from scorched Mercury to the cold blue edge of Neptune.

    Not to scale. Distances are compressed, close to the square root of each orbit, and small worlds are enlarged far more than giants, so all eight fit in one view; their order outward from the Sun is true.
